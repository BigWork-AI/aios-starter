# What stays out of this brain

The brain holds the business: customers, services, prices, meetings, decisions, promises, methods,
and the business's money: sales, costs, margins, invoices, what is owed, forecasts. The more of the
business it can see, the better its work.

It never holds: bank or card numbers, passwords or keys, tax or ID numbers, contracts you have not
chosen to share, health or family matters, anything about a person that they would not expect a
business to keep.

Payroll and what people are paid, your own pay included: your call. The brain asks once and writes
your answer here. Those live in the private folder at `%%PRIVATE%%`, outside this repository. The
brain never opens that folder, never indexes it and never asks for its contents.

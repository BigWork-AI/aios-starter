# What stays out of this brain

The brain holds the business: customers, services, prices, meetings, decisions, promises, methods.

It never holds: bank or card details, payroll, tax filings, contracts you have not chosen to share,
passwords or keys, health or family matters, anything about a person that they would not expect a
business to keep. Those live in the private folder at `%%PRIVATE%%`, outside this repository. The
brain never opens that folder, never indexes it and never asks for its contents.

- (none yet)

If something private gets in by mistake: stop, tell BigWork the same day, and do not push. Removal
means rewriting the saved history, not just deleting the file, and BigWork has the procedure.

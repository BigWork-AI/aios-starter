# What the brain may do without asking

The connections you give Claude decide what the brain *can* reach. This page decides what it *may
do* there on its own. You change it any time in plain words: "you can always do that" adds a line,
"stop doing that" takes one off. The brain reads this page before every action outside the brain.

## Allowed without asking

Each line is one kind of action, in one place, with its limits. "When I am away" means scheduled
jobs may do it too, with nobody watching.

## Everything else: it asks first

Anything not on the list above is drafted and shown to you. It acts on your yes, for that one time.

## Never, even if a line above seems to allow it

- Spend, pay, refund or move money.
- Delete anything outside the brain: emails, files, calendar events, records.
- Touch banking, accounting, payroll or pay.
- Change passwords, security settings, or who something is shared with.
- Open the private folder.

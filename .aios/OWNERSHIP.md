# Who owns what

Plain-English statement of intent for the friends-week pilot. A licence document replaces this
before the first paid install.

| Part | Owner | If we part ways |
|---|---|---|
| Everything about your business: `company/`, `clients/`, `memory/`, `inbox/`, `skills/` written for you | You | Keeps working. Take it anywhere. |
| The engine: `.aios/` (interview, playbooks, tools, templates, hooks) | BigWork AI, licensed to you | The installed version keeps working with your own AI account. Updates stop. |
| Tuning, support, new capabilities, hosting of anything always-on | BigWork AI, as a paid service | Stops. |
| Your AI account and connectors | You, with the provider | Unchanged. |

Nothing in this brain phones home to BigWork. BigWork sees your brain only when you invite a
BigWork account into your repository, and you can remove it any time.

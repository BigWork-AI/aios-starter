# voice

**Use when:** anything is drafted for the owner to send or post: emails, replies, quotes, posts,
proposals. Every other skill that drafts reads this first.
**Good result:** the owner reads a draft and says "that sounds like me", and changes little.
**Never:** invent phrases the owner does not use, or copy anyone's private words into a draft.

# What the brain did outside the brain

Every send, post, booking, acceptance or change the brain makes in a connected account is written
here the moment it happens, newest on top. One line each: what, where, to whom, and which
permission allowed it (a line on the permissions page, or your yes in the chat). No message
bodies, nothing private: a one-line summary is enough to check it.
